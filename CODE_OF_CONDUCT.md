# Project conduct

eMule Next welcomes lawful technical collaboration from people of every
background and experience level.

Participants must be respectful, patient and constructive. Harassment,
threats, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, spam and deliberate
disruption are not accepted. Critique the work, not the person.

Project spaces must not be used to distribute copyrighted media, unlawful
content, credentials, personal data, malicious code, deceptive downloads or
instructions intended to harm third parties. Security research must use the
private process in `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and
its community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented
privately where appropriate, and avoid exposing a reporter's personal data.

Questions about this policy may be raised through a GitHub issue as long as no
private or security-sensitive information is included.
